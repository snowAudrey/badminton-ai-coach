Badminton AI Coach

Turns short (under 1 minute) raw amateur badminton video into timestamped, technique-specific coaching feedback.

View on GitHub

Why

For amateur sports enthusiasts, access to a personal coach is often out of reach because it can be too expensive or simply unavailable. As a badminton player myself, I wanted feedback on my technique whenever I played, not just when I could find a coach. This project is meant as a training aid that supplements coaching rather than replaces it. The feedback it produces is a starting point for reflection instead of a substitute for a coach's judgment. It's also deliberately built for short clips: reviewing one or two rallies at a time keeps the feedback focused and actionable, so you can improve a little each session rather than being overwhelmed by everything at once.

What it sees & what it says

A skeleton is tracked on the selected player frame by frame. Each shot is found and named from that data, and the feedback cites the shot's time and measurements.

How it works

1

See

YOLOv8 detects the players and a custom-trained model tracks the shuttlecock. MediaPipe extracts 33 body keypoints per frame for the selected player.

2

Name the shots

Each shot is found from the racket wrist's speed, then named (smash, clear, drop, lift, net shot, block, serve) from where the racket hand was at contact and how the shuttle flew.

3

Retrieve

Each shot's name and measurements become search queries against a knowledge base of 2,090 coaching facts extracted from 117 tutorial videos (ChromaDB).

4

Coach

Claude writes timestamped feedback grounded strictly in the retrieved facts — naming several plausible shot types when the data is ambiguous instead of guessing confidently.

Honest limitations

  • Shuttle detection is sensitive to lighting, background clutter, and camera angle on amateur footage.
  • Very soft net shots by professionals barely move the wrist and can be missed. Forehand vs. backhand isn't identified yet.
  • Tested on ~15–34 second clips so far; feedback quality is a starting point for reflection, not a substitute for a coach's judgment.
